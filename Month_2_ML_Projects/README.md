# Month 2 – Regression & Classification

This folder contains **15 AI & Machine Learning projects** completed during **Month 2** of my portfolio.  
The focus of this month was **Regression, Classification, Hyperparameter Tuning, and Unsupervised Learning**, including practical projects and Kaggle competitions.

---

## 🗂️ Projects Overview

### **Week 1 – Regression**
1. **Predicting_Restaurant_Tips_using_Linear_&_Multiple_Regression.ipynb** – Linear & Multiple Regression analysis.  
2. **Regularization_Ridge_Lasso.ipynb** – Ridge & Lasso regularization techniques.  
3. **PolynomialRegression_enginesize_vs_co2_emission.ipynb** – Polynomial regression exploration.  
4. **Student_Marks_Prediction.ipynb** – Predicting student marks using regression.

### **Week 2 – Classification**
5. **Logistic_Regression_Titanic_survival.ipynb** – Logistic Regression on Titanic dataset.  
6. **Classification_DT_RF_titanic_dataset.ipynb** – Decision Tree & Random Forest classifier.  
7. **KNN&SVM_Predict_Student_Pass_Fail_.ipynb** – KNN & SVM for classification.  
8. **Hyperparameter_Tuning_Classifier_Comparison.ipynb** – Hyperparameter tuning and model evaluation.

### **Week 3 – Unsupervised Learning**
9. **High_Dimensional_Data_Visualization_Using_PCA.ipynb** – PCA for dimensionality reduction.  
10. **Unsupervised_Learning_Customer_Segmentation.ipynb** – Customer segmentation with K-Means.  
11. **Custom_Segmentation.ipynb** – Clustering and customer grouping.  
12. **DBSCAN_Advanced_Clustering__Customer_&_Synthetic_Data_Analysis.ipynb** – DBSCAN clustering analysis.  
13. **Unsupervised_Learning_–_Grouping_Project.ipynb** – Hierarchical clustering & visualization.

### **Week 4 – Kaggle Competitions / End-to-End ML**
14. **Classification_Titanic_Competition.ipynb** – Kaggle Titanic competition project.  
15. **End_to_End_ML_Regression_HousePrices.ipynb** – End-to-end regression project: data cleaning → model → evaluation.

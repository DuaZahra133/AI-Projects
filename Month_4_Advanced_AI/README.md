# Month 4 – Advanced AI

This folder contains **3 major AI projects** completed during **Month 4** of my portfolio.  
The focus of this month was on **Transfer Learning, Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), and Reinforcement Learning**, with practical applications in image classification, time series forecasting, and simple game simulations.

---

## 🗂️ Projects Overview

### **Project 1 – Transfer Learning (Image Classification)**
- **Image_Classification_Transfer_Learning.ipynb**  
  - Used pretrained models like **VGG16** and **ResNet50**.  
  - Fine-tuned models on small datasets (Flowers).  
  - Applied **data augmentation** to improve performance.  

### **Project 2 – RNN & Time Series Forecasting**
- **Time_Series_Forecasting_RNN.ipynb**  
  - Built **LSTM/GRU models** for stock price .  
  - Preprocessed time-series data, trained models, and evaluated using **RMSE & MAE**.  
  - Demonstrated optimization and hyperparameter tuning for better accuracy.  

### **Project 3 – Reinforcement Learning**
- **RL_Game_Simulation.ipynb**  
  - Implemented **Q-Learning** on simple game  Grid-world .  
  - Trained agent to learn optimal strategy.  
  - Visualized rewards and performance over episodes.  

> All projects are fully portfolio-ready and include code, explanations, and visualizations.
